Privacy

The short version: this site has no accounts, forms, analytics, adverts or tracking cookies. I don't collect your personal data through it.

Who I am

This is the personal website of Anish Achar, a student in Dublin, Ireland. For anything to do with your data, I'm the person responsible (the "controller" under the GDPR), and you can reach me at anishacharuw@gmail.com.

Cookies and what's stored on your device

The site sets no cookies. It keeps two small notes in your own browser, which are never sent to me or anyone else:

  • motion (local storage): remembers whether you switched animations on or off with the Motion switch. It stays until you clear your browser's data for this site.
  • intro (session storage): remembers that you've seen the opening animation, so it only plays once per visit. It's deleted when you close the tab.

Both exist only to make features you use work, so no consent banner is needed.

Hosting

Like every website, this one is delivered by a web host. When your browser asks for a page, the host necessarily receives your IP address and basic request details (such as your browser type and the time), and may keep them in short-lived security logs. I don't use those logs to identify or track anyone.

No other companies

Everything the site needs, including its fonts and code libraries, is served from the site itself. Your browser doesn't contact Google, a font service, an analytics company or anyone else while you're here. It only leaves the site if you follow a link, such as the one to LinkedIn.

Ghost Ball (the pool app)

Photos you take or choose in Ghost Ball are processed entirely inside your browser. They're never uploaded, stored or seen by me. "Save image" saves the result straight to your own device.

If you email me

I'll receive your email address and whatever you write, through Gmail (Google). I use it only to reply to you and keep it only as long as the conversation needs. Under the GDPR my reason for this is my legitimate interest in answering messages sent to me.

Links to other sites

Links to LinkedIn and other sites take you outside this one, where their own privacy policies apply.

Your rights, and deleting your data

You can ask to see, correct or delete any personal data I hold about you, to restrict or object to how I use it, or to get a copy. In practice, the only data I could hold is emails you've sent me.

To ask for deletion, email anishacharuw@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". I'll delete your messages and confirm within one month.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, you can complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission.

Children

This site doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children, and there's nothing to sign up for.

Accessibility

I want everyone to be able to use this site, and I aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. So far:

  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link. Escape closes an open project. The 3D models (the engine and the fin can) turn with the arrow keys, and the Ghost Ball balls move with them.
  • Images have text descriptions, and the key trading charts come with tables of the same numbers.
  • Text on the main site meets AA contrast against its background.
  • The Ghost Ball video has subtitles built in and a written transcript under it.
  • The Motion switch at the top of the main site turns the animations off, and the site also follows your device's reduced-motion setting.

Known limits: the 3D model and the drag-the-balls demo are visual by nature. The text, renders and tables around them describe the same work.

If something doesn't work for you, please email me and I'll fix it.
